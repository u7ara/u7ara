I wanted somewhere to write that wasn't a feed. No character limits, no engagement
loop, no algorithm deciding who sees what. Just a page, some text, and a date.

## Why a personal site

Social platforms are rented land. The posts live in someone else's database, ranked
by someone else's rules, and they can disappear without notice. A personal site is
boring in the best way: it's a folder of files I control, and it will still render
in ten years.

## What goes here

Mostly short notes — things I figure out while building, films I can't stop thinking
about, and the occasional longer piece when I have something worth saying. If a post
looks unfinished, it probably is. That's the point of a notebook.

If you want to follow along, the RSS feed is generated at build time and lives at
`/rss.xml`.
